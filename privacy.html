Privacy Policy — Draw with Raphael

Effective 21 August 2026

Draft of 16 August 2026 — not yet published.

Draw with Raphael is a drawing app made by one person. This policy is short because the honest answer is short: the app collects nothing, and the only place your drawings ever go is this phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. Draw with Raphael has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no servers of its own. We never see your name, your email, your drawings, or anything else. The app works entirely offline.

What the app keeps

Everything the app remembers is kept for you and only for you: the name you gave your companion, the look of the panda and its room, which prompts you have finished, the days you drew, the souvenirs and items you have collected, your ink balance, your streak, and your settings.

It also keeps the drawings themselves — every picture you finish, saved as an image alongside the record of that sitting. Nothing in the app can delete a finished drawing. Deleting the app removes what is on the device; see the Backups section below.

What the app never records

Any judgement of your drawing. The app has no opinion about what you drew: it is never graded, scored, rated, timed against a clock, or compared with anyone else's. There is no such number to collect, because none is ever written down. The only thing the app checks is that there is a mark on the paper, and even that is only so the Finish button knows when to wake up.

Backups

The app does not sync anything to iCloud, and it has no account, so there is nothing of yours anywhere but the device in your hand.

Your device's own iCloud backup, if you have that switched on in iOS Settings, includes this app's data along with everything else on the phone. That is Apple's backup of your whole device, run by Apple under Apple's privacy terms — it is not something this app sends, and the developer has no access to it or any way to read it. Restoring a new phone from that backup restores what you had here.

If you set a new phone up from scratch rather than from a backup, the app starts fresh. Nothing is recoverable, because nothing was ever stored anywhere else.

Notifications

If you choose a daily drawing reminder, it is scheduled locally on your device by iOS. Nothing about it leaves your phone, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings.

Children

Draw with Raphael is rated 4+. It shows no ads, has no chat, no links out, no purchases, and collects no data from anyone — including children. A child's drawings are private to that child's own device in exactly the same way an adult's are.

The prompts and the art

Every drawing prompt and every piece of pixel art in the app is bundled inside it at build time. Nothing is fetched while you draw, and no connection is needed to use any part of the app.

Your drawings are yours

The pictures you make belong to you. The app claims no rights over them, does not use them to train anything, and has no mechanism to send them anywhere at all.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described in plain language in the App Store release notes.
